01 / About

Studying the geometry
of intelligence.

I am a Ph.D. student at the Southern University of Science and Technology, advised by Prof. Yi Guo and Prof. Quanying Liu.

My research focuses on understanding how the geometry and structure of learned representations influence generalization, transfer, and alignment across tasks, domains, artificial neural networks, and the human brain.

I am particularly interested in foundation models, multimodal learning, representation learning, learning theory, EEG, fMRI, and neuroscience-inspired AI.

02 / Projects

Selected projects

Research projects exploring neural foundation models, representation geometry, and the relationship between AI and biological intelligence.

EEG Foundation Model · 2026

FAME: Frequency-Balanced EEG Foundation Model

Investigated the persistent low-frequency bias of EEG foundation models and proposed a frequency-balanced masked autoencoding framework. The 1B-parameter model achieved state-of-the-art performance on 24 of 41 downstream EEG tasks.

Python Deep Learning EEG
View project ↗
Fine-tuning · 2026

Stable Parameter Subspaces for Transferable Neural Networks

Studied the geometry of parameter updates during fine-tuning across vision, language, EEG, and fMRI foundation models. Restricting adaptation to stable pretrained subspaces enabled parameter-efficient fine-tuning with only 0.2% trainable parameters.

Foundation Models PEFT Transfer
View project ↗
AI-Brain Alignment · 2026

Convergent Representation Geometry in AI and the Human Brain

Analyzed AI representations and human fMRI responses under shared visual stimuli. The project linked AI–AI alignment, AI–brain alignment, model generalization, and local intrinsic dimensionality.

Learning Theory · 2026

Representation Geometry and Generalization Theory

Developed a representation-based framework connecting embedding dimensionality, Wasserstein distributional convergence, and generalization error under finite-sample learning.
